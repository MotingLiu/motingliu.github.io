01 / Research

From association
to biological insight.

My research brings together clinical outcomes, microbial functions and epithelial responses to understand the biological context of inflammatory bowel disease.

01

Microbial metabolites & IBD prognosis

Investigating associations between faecal short-chain fatty acids and subsequent therapy intensification, with a particular focus on valeric acid and microbial functional profiles.

Clinical cohortsSCFAsSurvival analysis
02

Human organoids & epithelial responses

Using human colonic organoids and organoid-derived monolayers to study how microbial metabolites influence inflammatory responses and epithelial electrical resistance.

Human organoidsRNA-seqECIS
03

Spatial biology & postoperative recurrence

Exploring host transcription, bacterial signals and cellular neighbourhoods in ileal resection margins to investigate spatial features associated with Crohn's disease recurrence.

XeniumSpatial transcriptomicsCrohn's disease
04

Sphingolipids across the host–microbe interface

Integrating faecal lipid profiles, microbial genomic potential and host transcription to characterise sphingolipid-related changes across intestinal locations and inflammatory states.

LipidomicsMetagenomicsMulti-omics
